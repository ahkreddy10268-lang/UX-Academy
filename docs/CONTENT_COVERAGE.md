## Maturity

Five separate dimensions, rated basic / developing / complete from the content (no numeric confidence scores):

- **Explanation:** all nine level checks.
- **Evidence:** ≥ 2 primary/standard sources, ≥ 1 machine-verified, ≥ 2 evidence points, a debate.
- **Practice:** ≥ 3 quiz questions, questions at all three levels, ≥ 2 exercises, ≥ 3 flashcards.
- **Visual:** plate (L1), detailed diagram (L2), explorer, lab or calculator (L3).
- **Editorial:** published, every quality gate, no source awaiting verification, a verification date.
