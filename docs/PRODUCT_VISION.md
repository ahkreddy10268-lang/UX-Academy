# Product vision

## Purpose

UX Academy is a long-term personal knowledge and learning companion for the whole design discipline: the depth of an academic resource, the discoverability of an encyclopedia, the practicality of a professional toolkit and the engagement of an interactive learning platform.

## The promise

For any topic, help the learner: understand what it means and why it exists; learn its origin; understand how it works; see where it applies and how to implement it; know when *not* to use it and its limitations; compare it with related ideas; experiment with it; practise it; test understanding; and go deeper into the research.

We do **not** promise a list of every concept ever. We promise a systematic, expandable ecosystem with transparent completeness tracking (`/coverage`).

## Learner profiles

| Profile | Needs | Served by |
|---|---|---|
| **Beginner** — no background | Plain language, analogies, examples, vocabulary, a route | Beginner level, learning paths, glossary, labs |
| **Intermediate designer** | How to apply it; implementation; comparisons | Intermediate level, application sections, comparisons, workshops |
| **Professional** | Quick reference, formulas, decision criteria | Search, Formula Lab, when/when-not, misconceptions |
| **Researcher / expert** | Primary sources, evidence, debates, mathematics | Advanced level, evidence notes, debates, source registry, DOIs |

The owner is all four over time; the product must stay useful as their expertise grows.

## Principles

1. Accuracy over quantity. 2. Explain rather than summarise. 3. Beginner accessibility without expert simplification. 4. Evidence-based (theory ≠ guideline ≠ convention). 5. Connected knowledge. 6. Learning through practice. 7. Long-term scalability. 8. Excellent educational experience.

## Differentiation (why not just read NN/g or IxDF?)

- **Evidence labelling**: every entry states its evidence class and what the evidence does and does not support — the "evidence ledger" on the home page is the product's signature.
- **Correct classification**: a "law" is only called a law if it is one.
- **Measured, not asserted**: labs fit regression lines to *your* data; calculators are unit-tested.
- **Connected**: typed, explained relationships, prerequisites and a graph.
- **Personal & private**: notes, spaced review and progress, local-first and portable.

## Success criteria

- A beginner can learn a topic without leaving the academy for a simpler explanation.
- Every published claim of substance is traceable to a verified or honestly-labelled source.
- No planned topic is ever presented as content.
- The owner returns to it regularly (bookmarks, notes and spaced review get used).
- Coverage grows each session without lowering the quality gate.
